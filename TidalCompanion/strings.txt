PLUGIN_TIDALCOMPANION
	EN	Tidal Companion

PLUGIN_TIDALCOMPANION_DESC
	EN	Works with the TIDAL plugin and online music library integration. Lossy albums in your library play from their lossless Tidal copy, lossy albums can be found on Tidal, and artists' missing and new albums can be added from Tidal.

PLUGIN_TIDALCOMPANION_SWAP
	EN	Play lossless from Tidal

PLUGIN_TIDALCOMPANION_SWAP_DESC
	EN	When a lossy album (MP3, AAC and so on) is also in the library from your Tidal favorites, play the lossless Tidal tracks instead. This applies wherever its tracks are played: the album, a single track, an artist, a playlist or a mix.

PLUGIN_TIDALCOMPANION_SCAN
	EN	Add new releases during library scans

PLUGIN_TIDALCOMPANION_SCAN_DESC
	EN	During each library scan, add albums that have come out on Tidal since the last check, for every album artist in your library, to your Tidal favorites so they appear in the library. The first scan only notes what each artist already has. To add an artist's older albums, use "Add missing albums from Tidal" on the artist.

PLUGIN_TIDALCOMPANION_SCAN_PROGRESS
	EN	New releases on Tidal
